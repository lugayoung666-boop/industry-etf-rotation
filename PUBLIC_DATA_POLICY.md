# Public data policy

This branch is a portfolio-safe, data-free publication of an internship research project.

## Included

- Strategy logic for 252-trading-day momentum signals.
- Point-in-time admission controls and fail-closed audits.
- The pre-declared RankIC and Top-minus-Bottom promotion gate.
- Unit tests, frozen configurations, and aggregate research statistics.

## Deliberately excluded

- Licensed Wind market data and industry constituent histories.
- Raw or processed daily price files.
- Full daily NAV, position, transaction, and security-level ranking histories.
- Employer-internal documents, local paths, credentials, tokens, and proprietary research materials.

The files under `reports/` contain aggregate statistical outputs only. They are provided to document the research conclusion, not to redistribute a market-data dataset.
